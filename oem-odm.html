OEM Process

How an OEM Project Runs

A typical development cycle from first contact to first shipment takes 4–10 weeks depending on complexity.

PHASE 01

Requirement & Quote

Share drawings, samples or target OE numbers. We evaluate feasibility and quote tooling + unit cost.

PHASE 02

Sample Development

Prototype samples produced and shipped for your validation and fitment testing.

PHASE 03

Branding & Packaging

Your logo, labels and packaging design applied after sample approval.

PHASE 04

Mass Production

Batch production with full QC reports, loading supervision and export documents.
