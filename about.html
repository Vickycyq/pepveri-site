Who We Are

Your Auto Part Business Partner in China

Pepveri Auto Parts is a B2B supplier headquartered in Shenzhen, China. We offer a wide product range covering engine parts, chassis parts, electrical parts, body parts and wear parts for all vehicle makes — mainly exported to the Middle East, South America, North America and Africa.

Our business model is simple: we work directly with certified factories, control quality at the source, and deliver quality parts at competitive prices to importers, wholesalers, distributors and repair chains worldwide.

  • 9 product categories covering engine, chassis, electrical, body and wear parts
  • Focus on popular pickups & SUVs: Japanese, American and Chinese models
