About PepVeri Biotech

A peptide raw-material manufacturer dedicated to supplying high-purity research powders to laboratories and cosmetic brands worldwide — pairing industrial-scale synthesis with the analytical rigor modern procurement demands.

Our Journey & Standards

Where Manufacturing Scale Meets Analytical Precision

PepVeri was founded on a simple conviction: buyers of peptide raw materials should never have to choose between production capacity and purity discipline. From our ISO 9001-aligned, GMP-standard facility, we have grown into a trusted supply partner for research laboratories, testing institutes and skincare brands on six continents.

We concentrate on two families of products — biochemical research peptides and active cosmetic ingredients — covering incretin sequence standards, reference materials such as BPC-157, and cosmetic actives across the GHK-Cu and Matrixyl families. Every synthesis cycle is supervised by a core team of PhD-level chemists and biochemists, from sequence design through final crystallization and release.

No batch leaves our factory without a complete analytical dossier. That rule has not changed since day one.
