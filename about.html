About PepVeri

A peptide supplier built around the part that usually goes wrong.

PepVeri connects overseas research groups with audited peptide manufacturing in China — and takes responsibility for everything between the sequence and the shipment.

Our story

A peptide service company — not a factory, but the partner who answers for your order

PepVeri is a supplier and service provider, not a manufacturer. We keep an in-stock catalog of 300+ research peptides ready to ship, and when a project needs something the catalog does not cover, we place the work with manufacturing partners we have audited ourselves — for licence scope, equipment and batch-record discipline — and manage the project through to delivery.

That division of labour is deliberate. Our job is not to run a reactor; it is to make sure the sequence you need arrives on time, at the purity you were promised, with the CoA, MSDS and customs paperwork in the box. The feasibility review, supplier qualification, QC sign-off and every conversation sit with our own team, so you deal with one company that answers for the whole chain rather than a mailbox that forwards to a factory.

In short: for standard items, buy off the shelf and receive documented, in-stock material in days. For custom work, get a difficult sequence taken from the first feasibility question to a cold-chain shipment — produced by vetted partners, released by us.

How we work

Five verbs we are willing to be judged on

Every order — a single catalog vial or a multi-gram custom batch — moves through the same five stages. Nothing is released on trust alone.

01

Source

Resins, amino acids and reagents bought from qualified suppliers, with incoming CoA review and batch traceability on file.

02

Produce

Synthesis at audited partner sites, run to a route and specification we approve — not to whatever their default protocol happens to be.

03

Qualify

HPLC and LC-MS on every batch; NMR, amino-acid analysis and impurity profiling when your specification calls for it.

04

Stock

Fast-moving catalog items held in temperature-controlled inventory, so repeat orders ship in days rather than weeks.

05

Deliver

CoA, MSDS and customs paperwork prepared per order, packed for cold chain and shipped with tracking.
