FAQ

Questions research teams ask us first

What information do you need for a quote?
Paste your sequence (single-letter or FASTA), tell us the peptide type, target purity, quantity and any modifications. You will receive a quote within one business day. NDA available before you share anything sensitive.
What purities and scales do you offer?
From crude to >98% purity, at scales from 1 mg to gram quantities. Bulk and peptide library projects are welcome — pricing improves with volume.
How do you protect my sequence and project data?
Confidentiality is our default working mode: we sign NDAs on request, restrict sequence access internally, and never reuse or resell your sequences or project information.
How do you ship, and what documentation is included?
Temperature-controlled worldwide shipping. Every order includes HPLC & MS reports (CoA); MSDS and additional batch documentation are available. All products are supplied strictly for research use only.
